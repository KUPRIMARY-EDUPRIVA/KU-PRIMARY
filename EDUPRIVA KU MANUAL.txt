EDUPRIVA KU
USER MANUAL

Version: October 2026

======================================================================
1. ABOUT THIS MANUAL
======================================================================

This manual explains how to sign in, navigate the Edupriva KU school
management app, use the main school modules, export reports, and get
help with common issues. Your available pages and actions depend on
your account role and the features enabled for your school.

The Android app has a native Capacitor shell with the application
interface bundled on the device. It connects to Firebase for school
data and to the school's Netlify functions for supported server-side
operations.

======================================================================
2. SIGNING IN AND OFFLINE ACCESS
======================================================================

First sign-in
-------------
1. Connect the device to the internet.
2. Open Edupriva KU and enter the email address and password registered
   for your school account.
3. Select the sign-in button and wait for the application to load your
   role and school information.

Offline use
-----------
The first sign-in requires an internet connection. After signing in,
the app may use the saved session and cached information when offline.
Some protected offline sessions require biometric unlock. Biometric
access can be enabled when offered by the app; the app does not store
your password for biometric access.

Offline access is limited. Actions that require a live server, including
some exports, SMS, account-management actions, and payment-related
operations, need an internet connection. Changes queued by an offline
feature may not appear to other users until they synchronize. Check the
connection/sync indicator before relying on recently entered data.

For password recovery, connect to the internet and use the sign-in
screen's password recovery option. Contact your school administrator if
you cannot access the registered email account.

======================================================================
3. ROLES AND ACCESS
======================================================================

The application shows different navigation items according to role.
Some modules are also controlled by school feature settings.

Admin
-----
- School-wide dashboard and management pages, including students,
  teachers, exams, results, fee management, attendance reports,
  school performance reports, student reports, school profile,
  timetables, and enabled optional modules.
- Attendance is for school-wide statistics, filters, history and
  reports; the Admin does not mark individual students present or absent.
- Does not have the Discipline navigation tab.
- Can edit the school profile and manage school administrator accounts.

HeadTeacher
-----------
- Has the Admin management pages.
- Also has Communications and Discipline.
- Attendance is the school-wide reporting view, not attendance marking.
- Can edit the school profile and manage school administrator accounts.

Deputy-Headteacher
------------------
- Has the teacher dashboard and teacher pages, plus Attendance,
  Student Reports and Discipline.
- Can mark attendance only for classes assigned to the account as class
  teacher.
- Does not have school-profile editing or administrator-management
  access.

Teacher
-------
- Uses the teacher dashboard, assigned students/classes, results,
  attendance, reports, timetable and profile pages as made available.
- Attendance marking is restricted to assigned classes.

Accountant
----------
- Uses Fee Management only. Other school modules are not part of the
  Accountant navigation.

Student
-------
- Uses the student dashboard, own results and own fee information.

Super Administrator
-------------------
- Uses platform-level administration pages to manage schools, users and
  platform settings. This role is different from a school's Admin.

======================================================================
4. NAVIGATION AND BASIC USE
======================================================================

1. Sign in. The navigation panel is available from the app's menu.
2. Choose a page from the sidebar or mobile navigation.
3. Select the relevant school year, term, level, class, subject or date
   before loading records where those filters are provided.
4. Use Save/Apply/Load actions to commit or retrieve information.
5. Use the page's export action to generate a report or data file.
6. Check the application notice and the device's Downloads/files area
   after an export. A successful export should produce a file; a notice
   by itself is not the exported document.

On mobile, less-frequently used pages may be placed under the More menu.
If a page is not visible, check your account role, school feature
settings, and the More menu.

======================================================================
5. SCHOOL PROFILE AND ADMINISTRATORS
======================================================================

Only the school's Admin and HeadTeacher can edit the school profile or
manage school administrator accounts. The profile contains settings
such as school contact details, academic configuration, class-teacher
assignments, finance configuration, administrator accounts, and
preferences. Save changes after editing.

Adding an administrator
-----------------------
1. Open School Profile, then the Admins tab.
2. Select Add Admin.
3. Enter the person's registered account email, name and phone number.
4. Choose the appropriate role and submit.
5. The person must already have a registered user account. If the email
   cannot be found, ask the person to register/sign in first, then retry.

Role limits are:
- Admin: at most 1
- HeadTeacher: at most 1
- Deputy-Headteacher: at most 2
- Accountant: at most 2

If the limit for a role has been reached, an existing role holder must
be removed before another account can be assigned that role. The
backend is intended to enforce these limits as well as the role selector.

Deleting an administrator
-------------------------
Removing an administrator is a two-step process:
1. An Admin or HeadTeacher requests deletion from the administrator list.
2. The account being removed receives an approval button while that
   request is active. The account owner must sign in and approve.
3. After approval, the system deletes the person's Firebase sign-in
   account and school user record. They are signed out and must no longer
   be able to use that account.

The approval button is only shown while a deletion request is pending.
Do not request deletion unless the account owner is expected to review
and approve it. A replacement for a one-person role can be assigned
after the existing account has been deleted.

IMPORTANT: These administrator-management safeguards require the
corresponding Netlify function and Firestore rules to be deployed to the
live environment. If administrator assignment or approval is unavailable,
contact the platform operator rather than attempting to edit user roles
directly.

======================================================================
6. STUDENTS AND TEACHERS
======================================================================

Student management
------------------
Authorized school staff can use Students to maintain student records
and view/search the student roster. Use the student's admission number
when searching or importing records. Admission numbers shorter than
four digits are normalized with leading zeros for matching; for example,
45 can match 0045. Confirm the matched student's name and class before
saving financial or academic information.

Use the page's own actions for editing, importing, promotion, transfer,
graduation or archiving. Review the destination level and class before
confirming promotions, especially when processing a group.

Teacher management
------------------
Authorized administrators use Teachers to maintain teacher profiles
and assignments. Keep each teacher's classes, levels and subjects
current; those assignments control the teacher's access to class
attendance and academic work.

Class-teacher assignment
------------------------
In School Profile, use Class Teachers to associate each class with its
responsible teacher. A teacher who is not assigned to a class may not be
able to mark attendance for it.

======================================================================
7. ATTENDANCE
======================================================================

Class teachers and Deputies
---------------------------
1. Open Attendance.
2. Select an assigned class, session and date.
3. Review the student roster. Use Mark all present/absent only when
   appropriate, then correct individual student statuses and add
   optional notes.
4. Select Save Attendance.
5. Use History to filter saved records by class and date range.

Admin and HeadTeacher
---------------------
The school-wide attendance view is reports-only. Select the available
date, term, class or other report filters, review the statistics and
generate the report. Administrators do not use the class marking
controls.

Attendance reports can be exported as PDF. Allow the report to finish
generating, then open the PDF from the device's Downloads or file
location. If no file appears, retry while online and check the
application's error notice.

======================================================================
8. EXAMS, RESULTS AND STUDENT REPORTS
======================================================================

Exams
-----
Use Exams to configure and manage assessment/exam information available
to your role. Confirm the term, level, class and assessment details
before publishing or changing an exam.

Entering results
----------------
1. Open Results.
2. Choose the school level, class, subject, term and assessment type.
   The level list is limited to the highest level configured for the
   school.
3. Select Load Students and verify the roster.
4. Enter or import scores. For CSV imports, check the required column
   headers shown on the page and ensure each admission number identifies
   the intended student.
5. Save the scores and confirm that the save completes.

Administrators can open or close result entry for assessment types at
each configured school level. Closed entry is read-only for teachers.
The lock controls do not list levels above the school's configured
highest level.

Results can provide score-sheet/ranking exports in CSV and student or
ranking PDFs. Choose the correct class and term before exporting.

Student Reports and performance reports
---------------------------------------
Use Student Reports for individual student report forms and School
Performance Reports for school-level summaries. Apply the term, year,
level and class filters, review the displayed data, then select Export
PDF. Student report access depends on the account role.

======================================================================
9. FEE MANAGEMENT
======================================================================

Finding a student
-----------------
Use the live search controls in Fee Management. Search by admission
number or switch to name search. Enter at least two characters to search.
Admission-number search normalizes leading zeros, so 45 can match 0045.
Verify the student's name, admission number and class before recording
or viewing a transaction.

Common fee tasks
----------------
- Review fee balances, invoices and invoice statistics.
- Record a payment or other permitted fee transaction against the
  correct student.
- Create or review invoices and fee structures where available.
- Open a student's fee details for transaction and balance information.
- Generate a receipt after a payment and save/print the resulting PDF.
- Use reconciliation actions when a balance needs to be checked against
  its transactions.
- Export defaulters or other data only after reviewing the current term,
  year and filters.

For M-Pesa or online payment activity, allow the payment callback to
complete before treating a transaction as final. If the student's
balance does not update, refresh the page and ask an authorized user to
reconcile the balance. Do not enter the same payment a second time
without checking the transaction history.

PDF receipts and other fee documents depend on the required server-side
services when applicable. Stay online for payment, reminder and
server-generated operations. After selecting a PDF action, verify that
the file is actually present in the device's Downloads/files location.

======================================================================
10. DISCIPLINE AND COMMUNICATIONS
======================================================================

Discipline is available to the HeadTeacher and Deputy-Headteacher.
Authorized users can find a student by admission number or by entering
at least two letters of the student's name.

To record a case:
1. Select the correct student and verify their identity.
2. Enter the incident date, category, severity, summary and action taken.
3. Add a follow-up date if needed, then save the record.
4. Use the status controls to track whether a case is open, under review
   or resolved.
5. Filter existing records by student, class, severity, status and date.
6. Export the filtered records and statistics to PDF when required.

Parent/guardian SMS is a confidential follow-up asking the parent to
contact the school; incident details are not included in the message.
Verify that the parent's phone number is correct before sending.
Discipline information is sensitive: access and share reports only with
authorized school personnel.

Communications is available to the HeadTeacher. Review recipients and
message content carefully before sending SMS or other communications.
Some communications require an internet connection and an active SMS
service.

11. OTHER SCHOOL MODULES
======================================================================

Depending on role and school configuration, additional pages may include:
- School Performance Reports: filtered school-level academic summaries.
- Timetable: view/manage timetable information permitted for the role.
- Student Transcripts: student academic-history records.
- Transport: buses, routes and related transport records.
- Accommodation/Boarding: dormitories and boarding records.
- Health Records: authorized student health information.
- Inventory: school inventory records.
- Subscription: school subscription information and support.
- Audit Logs: review recorded administrative activity.
- Settings/My Profile: personal account preferences and profile details.

Use each page's on-screen filters and prompts. Do not assume that a
hidden navigation item is a system error; it may not be available to
your role or may be disabled for your school.

======================================================================
12. EXPORTS AND FILES
======================================================================

PDF and CSV exports are generated per module and may use different
filters. Before exporting:
1. Check the displayed school, term, year, class, dates and student.
2. Apply the desired filters and confirm the on-screen results.
3. Select the export action once and wait for completion.
4. Open the device's Downloads or file manager and locate the generated
   file. On a native Android device, file access/save prompts may appear.
5. Open the document to confirm that it contains the expected records.

If the app reports an export error, note the page, filters, time and
error text. Retry online. If the message reports success but the file is
missing, check Downloads and the app's requested save location before
repeating the export; contact support if the file still cannot be found.

======================================================================
13. PRIVACY, SECURITY AND GOOD PRACTICE
======================================================================

- Use your own account and do not share your password or device unlock.
- Lock the device when unattended and sign out on shared devices.
- Use only the role assigned to you; never ask another person to approve
  a deletion request on your behalf.
- Verify student identity before editing marks, fees, attendance or
  discipline information.
- Treat exported student, financial, health and discipline reports as
  confidential. Store and share them only through approved channels.
- Do not upload spreadsheets containing unrelated personal data.
- Keep admission numbers, classes, dates and terms consistent in imports.
- A server-side feature may require internet access even when other
  cached parts of the app remain available offline.

======================================================================
14. TROUBLESHOOTING
======================================================================

Cannot sign in
--------------
Check internet access, email spelling and password. First sign-in must
be online. Use password recovery or contact the school administrator.

Page or action is missing
-------------------------
Confirm the account role and ask an Admin/HeadTeacher whether the school
feature is enabled. Some navigation links are intentionally role-limited.

Teacher cannot mark a class
---------------------------
Ask an administrator to assign the teacher as class teacher for that
class in School Profile. Refresh after the assignment is saved.

Student search returns no match
-------------------------------
Check the admission number, school and student status. Try at least two
characters for live search; a short admission such as 45 should match
0045 when the stored number uses four digits.

PDF or CSV file is missing
--------------------------
Check Downloads and file-save prompts. Confirm filters and connectivity.
Retry only after checking that the same file was not already saved.

Fee balance or payment looks incorrect
--------------------------------------
Refresh and inspect the student's transactions/invoices. Do not
duplicate a payment. Ask an authorized fee manager to reconcile the
balance and provide the transaction reference to support.

Deletion approval button does not appear
-----------------------------------------
The approval button appears only for the account targeted by an active
deletion request. The account owner must be signed in and online for the
app to retrieve the request. Contact the requesting administrator if
there is no pending request.

Changes do not synchronize
--------------------------
Restore internet access and wait for the sync indicator. Reopen the page
and verify the server-side value before entering the change again.

======================================================================
15. SUPPORT CHECKLIST
======================================================================

When reporting an issue, provide:
- Your role and school name/ID.
- The page and action you were using.
- The date/time and whether the device was online.
- The exact error text, if shown.
- Relevant student admission number or transaction reference only
  through an approved private support channel.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment secrets or private
student records in an unsecured message.
